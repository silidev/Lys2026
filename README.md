Keeping essential food and other items well-stocked at home and shopping for them always stressed me out because it was so chaotic. Giving this process a clear structure helped a lot. I've been using a self-developed app for it over the past year or two, and I've been super happy with it ever since. All important items are shown first in the order you want to check them at home, and then in the order you walk through the supermarket.

It has no ads and is forever completely free and open source.
